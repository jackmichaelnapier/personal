Station 02 · The Rope Bench

Bundles, true to the math.

Bundles are the easy win. Buying three things at a discount really is cheaper than buying them one by one — so the discount display stays. We just make the math behind it honest.

Three barrels for the price of two and a half is a real bargain.
The work

One pass through every bundle.

Step by step

For each bundle on the site.

01

Walk every bundle

One sweep through all of them.

02

Set regular price = sum of components

Add up the regular price of every item in the bundle. That's the bundle's regular price.

03

Set sales price = what the customer actually pays

The bundle price. The display calculates the saving on its own.

A worked example

3 × Whey Protein bundle.

The math

How €15 of saving is built.

Three Whey at €30 each, bought individually
3 × €30 = €90
The bundle price the customer actually pays
€75
Real saving, every time
€15 · 17%

Because the regular price is the real sum of the components, the percentage stops being a marketing trick and starts being a fact. Customer can check the math themselves.

💡
A note from the workshop

Bundles are the only place in the catalog where "save 17%" is mathematically true by construction. Everywhere else, claiming a saving means defending a prior price you actually charged. Here, the prior price is just addition. Boring, but bullet-proof.

Try this, before you scroll on

Pick one bundle and run the numbers.

Sum the regular prices of its components. Does it equal the bundle's current "regular" price on the site? That gap is the size of the work for that bundle.
