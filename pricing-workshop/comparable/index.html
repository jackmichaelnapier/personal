Station 03 · The Scholar's Bench

Comparable, not invented.

We want shoppers to see we beat the competition on price. The clear way: show "Our price" next to a real competitor's price, with a source. No fake "regular" price, no fake percentage. Just a number you can stand behind.

A price next to a real rival's price tells a story. A fake percentage tells a tale.

Sourcing the comparable price

Four rules per SKU.

The standard

What makes a comparable price defensible.

✓

Source from the highest-priced Danish competitors — BodyLab, Bodystore, or a specialty store, and other comparable Danish supplement sites.

✓

The competitor product must be genuinely comparable — same active ingredient, similar serving size, similar form.

✓

Keep a record per product: comparable price, source site + URL, date checked, comparability notes.

✓

Maintain one dedicated tracking sheet for all 150 SKUs. That sheet is the source of truth for what shows on the site.

Example record format

What a tracked SKU looks like.

SKU Comparable price Source Checked
FN-WHEY-VAN-1K €25.00 bodylab.dk/whey-100 15 May 2026
FN-CREA-MONO-500 €18.50 bodystore.dk/creatine 15 May 2026
🔁
A note from the workshop

The comparable price has a half-life. Set a quarterly review as a recurring ClickUp task: competitor product still exists? Price still accurate? Update the record. If a comparable can't be justified, drop the label until a new source is found.

Try this, before you scroll on

Find one comparable price right now.

Pick any private-label SKU. Open BodyLab in a tab. Find the closest equivalent. Write down the price, the URL, and today's date. That's one row of the sheet.
