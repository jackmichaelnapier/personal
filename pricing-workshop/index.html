The Why

Why we're doing any of this.

One paragraph before the stations. The shorter version is on every station too.

A real low price beats a fake discount, every time.
💡
A note from the workshop

Too many products on the site are wearing a fake discount: a "regular" price we never actually charged, a "SPAR X%" badge that's been there for months. Customers see through it eventually, and the rules in Denmark are clear about it. Clear pricing makes our value story stronger, not weaker.

The 30-second version

If you only read one thing.

Flash sale

Only genuinely short-term offers. Log every deal in the ops sheet.

Bundles

Keep the discount display. Make the math real.

Top 150 SKUs

"Our price" vs "Comparable price". Sourced, clear, no fake %.

Everything else

Drop the regular/sale/save framing. A single price wins.
