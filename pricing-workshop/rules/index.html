Station 04 · The Standing Stone

Rules, cheat sheet, finish line.

The work in Stations 01-03 changes this quarter. The principles below outlast the project. When in doubt, walk back to the stone and read it again.

The five principles

Memorise these. Most edge-cases answer themselves.

1

The 30-day rule for the prior price

When we show a price reduction, the "prior price" must be our own lowest price in the previous 30 days. Not a market reference, not a competitor price.

Don't dress up a stable price as a discount

If there's been no real reduction from our own prior price: no "save X%", no strikethrough, no before/now.

2
3

Flash sale = genuinely short-term

No automatic extensions. No rolling discounts on the same products month after month.

Comparative pricing has rules

Comparing to a competitor is allowed only with a transparent source, a genuinely comparable product, ongoing accuracy, and no discount percentages.

4
5

Long-term low? Use "fixed low price"

Permanent or long-running low prices are framed as "fixed low price", never as a discount.

The finish line

You'll know we're done when…

Tick these off and we ship.

Definition of done

Six boxes to tick.

Every non-flash-sale product shows a single price: no strikethrough, no save badge.

Every bundle's regular price equals the sum of its component regular prices.

Top 50 PL + top 100 third-party SKUs are labeled with "Sammenlignelig pris" / "LAVPRIS" and have records in the tracking sheet.

Flash sale page contains only genuinely short-term offers. Everything else has moved to Tilbud.

The flash sale ops sheet is created and active, with a realistic regular price logged for every flash sale deal.

Quarterly review is scheduled in ClickUp as a recurring task.

A real low price beats a fake discount, every time.
