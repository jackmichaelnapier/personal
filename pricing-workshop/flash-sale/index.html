Station 01 · The Lightning Bench

Flash sale, only flashing.

Right now the flash sale page is doing too much work. Items sit there for weeks. Some never leave. By the end of this station the page only shows things that are genuinely on a short-term offer.

Lightning that lingers isn't lightning. It's wallpaper.
The work

Four steps. Top to bottom.

Step by step

What you'll actually do.

01

Audit the page

Look at every product currently sitting on flash sale. Make a list.

02

Remove anything that isn't a real short-term offer

If it's been there for weeks, it's not flash sale — it's wallpaper. Take it off.

03

Reset the prices on removed items

Copy sales_price into regular_price, then clear sales_price. Example: regular €10 / sales €5 becomes regular €5, no sales price.

04

Removed products live on the Tilbud page

With their new single price. No strikethrough, no save badge.

Real flash vs wallpaper flash.

Real flash

Lasts hours or a weekend

Genuinely time-limited. When the window closes, the product comes off the page.

Wallpaper

Lasts weeks or months

Permanent stickers customers stop noticing and regulators don't. Not flash, just always-on.

📜
House rules going forward

Flash sale means genuinely time-limited offers only. No automatic extensions. Permanent or long-running low prices belong on the Tilbud page with "fixed low price" framing.

Try this, before you scroll on

Count the wallpaper.

Open the flash sale page. How many items have been on it for more than two weeks? That number is the size of Step 02.
