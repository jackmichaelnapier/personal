001 — What we build

Three pieces of plumbing. One quiet operation.

Most small businesses don't need another dashboard. They need the systems they already use to talk to each other, the work nobody loves to keep current to keep itself current, and a weekly snapshot honest enough to act on.

// 01

Custom MCP servers

Bespoke connectors that plug your stack into Claude — and the rest of your team's AI. Audited, documented, and hosted on your account so the data never leaves.

  • ClickUp, Slack, Drive, Gmail
  • BigQuery & Postgres reads
  • Internal tools & private APIs
  • Trigger events into your workflow
// 02

Scheduled agents

Routines that wake up so you don't have to. Cron-driven Claude agents that gather, reconcile, and post — on the schedule that matches your operation, not someone else's product roadmap.

  • Daily revenue snapshots
  • Weekly digests & KPI rollups
  • End-of-month reconciliations
  • Compliance & data-freshness alerts
// 03

Weekly reports

Numbers that look you in the eye every Monday. Self-contained HTML, password-gated, archived — the kind of report you actually open because it was made for your team, not a generic template.

  • Revenue, MTD, YTD vs LY
  • Marketing spend & ROAS cuts
  • Purchasing & compliance digests
  • Posts straight into Slack or ClickUp
scheduled-agent · monday 07:00 CET
# Every Monday at 07:00, before the team is at their desks
agent "weekly-overview" {
  on: "0 7 * * 1",
  tools: ["bigquery", "clickup", "github-pages"],
  do: [
    pull("revenue.weekly_overview", week=last_completed),
    render("report.html", style="javea"),
    publish("go.example.com/reports/2026-w17"),
    post("#swiss_finance", "📊 Week 17 is live → {{ url }}")
  ]
}
