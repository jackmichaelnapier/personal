02 · Story

How I got here.

I came up running e-commerce operations across northern Europe — the unglamorous machinery behind a thousand small daily transactions. Pricing engines, shipping rules, BigQuery jobs at 4am, the kind of work where the wins are quiet and the losses are loud.

At some point I realised I'd rather make those decisions in flip-flops than under a northern sky. So I packed the operation onto a laptop and moved it to Jávea, on the Costa Blanca — a town of fishermen, foreigners, almond trees, and a sea that does most of my therapy for me.

The work didn't get smaller. The room around it just got better.

These days I split my time between the day job, building tooling that lets a small team punch above its weight, and hosting Build & Bike — a yearly excuse to bring people I admire to this corner of Spain to ride, ship, and eat properly.

I write less than I'd like, ship more than I should, and keep a running list of side projects that I will absolutely get to next quarter.
