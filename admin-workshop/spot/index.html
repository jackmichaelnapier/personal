Station 01 · The Sorting Bench

Spot the skill-shape.

Some admin tasks are skill-shaped, most aren't. By the end of this station, you'll be able to tell them apart in ten seconds and stop wasting your time on the ones that aren't ready.

A skill-shaped task has a clean input, a single verb, and a structured output.
📥

One input

A noun phrase. A supplier invoice. A SKU's sales history. A reviewer comment.

⚙️

One verb

A single transformation. Reconcile. Project. Classify. Not three at once.

📤

One output shape

Something you could schema. A list, a verdict, a structured object. Not "a paragraph."

🦆
Otto thinks…

If you can't write the input as a noun phrase, the verb as a single word, and the output as something you could draw on a piece of paper, the task isn't skill-shaped yet. That's fine — it just means it needs more thought before you wrap it in a skill.

Skill-shaped, or not yet.

Skill-shaped

Reconcile this invoice

Given a supplier invoice, the matching PO, and the GRN, reconcile line-by-line and flag discrepancies with a pay/hold/reject recommendation.

Not yet

Tighten up suppliers

No input. No defined transformation. No checkable output. A coaching conversation, not a skill.
