Station 02 · The Cutting Bench

Find the cut lines.

Procure-to-pay looks like one workflow. It's actually six output forms strung together. Cut where the form genuinely changes — not on activity boundaries, not on calendar boundaries.

Cut where the output form changes.
🦆
Otto thinks…

The Goldilocks rule. Cut too coarse, you get one fragile mega-skill that fails unpredictably and you can't debug. Cut too fine, you spend more time on handoffs than on work. Cut at form-change boundaries and the joins take care of themselves.

Worked example

The procure-to-pay flow, decomposed.

Ten activities. Four cuts. Five skills. Each cut sits where the output form genuinely changes.

1. identify low-stock SKUs · 2. forecast 90-day demand · 3. calc reorder qty │ CUT │ 4. issue PO to supplier │ CUT │ 5. track shipment · 6. receive goods + GRN │ CUT │ 7. 3-way invoice match · 8. approve / hold / reject │ CUT │ 9. update stock ledger · 10. reconcile bank statement

Each cut sits where the output form genuinely changes: planning number → PO commitment → logistics state → reconciliation verdict → ledger entry. Five skills, four cuts.

Before you scroll on

Pick your own workflow. Where are the form-change cuts?
