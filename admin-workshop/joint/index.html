Station 05 · The Joinery

The joints are where stacks fail.

Two skills can each be perfect and the stack still fails because what one produces isn't what the next can consume. Most ops stacks fail at the joints, not the nodes. Design them together.

Skill A's output schema must be Skill B's input schema.
