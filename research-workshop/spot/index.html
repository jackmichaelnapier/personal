Station 01 · The Sorting Bench

Spot the skill-shape.

Some research tasks are skill-shaped, most aren't. By the end of this station you'll be able to tell them apart in ten seconds and stop wasting your time on the ones that aren't ready.

A skill-shaped task has a clean input, a single verb, and a structured output.
📥

One input

A noun phrase. An XRD pattern. A list of papers. A reviewer comment + manuscript section.

⚙️

One verb

A single transformation. Identify. Classify. Fit. Not three at once.

📤

One output shape

Something you could schema. A phase + confidence. A gap list. A classified comment.

👩‍🔬
Marie thinks…

If you can't write the input as a noun phrase, the verb as a single word, and the output as something you could draw on a piece of paper, the task isn't skill-shaped yet. That's fine — it just means it needs more thought before you wrap it in a skill.

Skill-shaped, or not yet.

Skill-shaped

Identify this XRD phase

Given a powder XRD pattern and a shortlist of plausible phases, identify the dominant crystal phase with a confidence score and flag any peaks that don't match.

Not yet

Help me think about my next research direction

No input. No defined transformation. No checkable output. A great conversation, but not a skill.

What's a recurring research task in your week?
